How it works

Blokada 6 uses Blokada Cloud. The app points your device at a private, encrypted DNS service that leaves out ads and trackers, so they are never loaded. Nothing runs in the background and your battery is not affected.

One account covers all your devices. For devices that cannot run the app, such as routers, TVs and computers, the setup guides show how to use the same service.

What was blocked, and what you want to allow, is in the app and in the web dashboard.

How it works

Blokada 5 filters ads on the device itself, using Android's VPN feature. Your traffic is not sent to a VPN server. It is not on Google Play, so you install it from the download page.

Blokada 6 is the current app. It blocks in the cloud, runs on iPhone as well, and covers the other devices in your home.

What it costs

Blokada 5 is free. There is no trial, no account and no subscription.

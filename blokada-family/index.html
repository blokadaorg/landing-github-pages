How it works

Blokada Family is our dedicated app for families, designed to ensure a safe and secure online environment for your children. It allows parents to effectively manage and monitor internet access on all family devices.

With features like customizable content filters, activity monitoring, and safe search, Blokada Family helps protect your children from inappropriate content and online risks. It also includes tools to block social media and trackers, enhancing both safety and privacy.

Blokada Family integrates seamlessly with our other products, offering a comprehensive solution for family internet safety.

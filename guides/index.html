Blokada Cloud setup guides

Blokada Cloud blocks ads and trackers in DNS, so it works on devices that cannot run an ad blocker app. Pick your device or the service you are switching from.

  • A Pi-hole alternative that needs no hardwareMove your home's ad blocking from a Pi-hole to Blokada Cloud, or keep your Pi-hole and send its lookups through Blokada.
  • Mullvad DNS is shutting down. Keep your ad blocking with Blokada CloudMullvad closes its public DNS on 2 November 2026. Here is how to move your phone, computer and router to Blokada Cloud before then, without losing ad blocking.
  • Block ads on your whole network with router ad blockingSet up Blokada Cloud on your router once, and every device at home is covered, including TVs, game consoles and smart speakers that cannot run an ad blocker.
  • Block ads on Android with Private DNSUse Android's built-in Private DNS setting with Blokada Cloud to block ads and trackers in every app, on Wi-Fi and mobile data, with no app to install.
  • Block ads on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TVInstall a Blokada Cloud DNS profile to block ads and trackers system-wide on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV, with encrypted DNS and no app running.
  • Block ads in Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Brave with DNS over HTTPSSet Blokada Cloud as the secure DNS provider in your browser to block ads and trackers, on any computer, including work laptops where you can't install apps.

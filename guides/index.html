Blokada Cloud setup guides

Step-by-step guides to block ads and trackers on your router, TV, computer and phone with Blokada Cloud.

Blokada Cloud blocks ads and trackers in DNS, so it works on devices that cannot run an ad blocker app. Pick your device or the service you are switching from.

Set up a device

  • RouterRecommended: covers every device at home Set up Blokada Cloud on your router once, and every device at home is covered, including TVs, game consoles and smart speakers that cannot run an ad blocker.
  • Android Use Android's built-in Private DNS setting with Blokada Cloud to block ads and trackers in every app, on Wi-Fi and mobile data. Or let the Blokada 6 app do it.
  • Mac, iPhone and Apple TV Install a Blokada Cloud DNS profile to block ads and trackers system-wide on a Mac or Apple TV, with encrypted DNS and nothing running in the background.
  • Windows Use the encrypted DNS built into Windows 11 with Blokada Cloud to block ads and trackers in every app and browser, with no software to install.
  • Linux Set up systemd-resolved to use Blokada Cloud over encrypted DNS over TLS, and block ads and trackers for every app on your Linux computer.
  • Browsers Set Blokada Cloud as the secure DNS provider in your browser to block ads and trackers, on any computer, including work laptops where you can't install apps.

Switch from another service

  • From Mullvad DNSCloses 2 November 2026 Mullvad closes its public DNS on 2 November 2026. Here is how to move your phone, computer and router to Blokada Cloud before then, without losing ad blocking.
  • From Pi-hole Move your home's ad blocking from a Pi-hole to Blokada Cloud, or keep your Pi-hole and send its lookups through Blokada.
  • From NextDNS Move from NextDNS to Blokada Cloud. Swap your NextDNS DNS name, DoH link or profile for Blokada's on your phone, computer and router, and keep your ad blocking.
